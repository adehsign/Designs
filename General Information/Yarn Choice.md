#hi
you want sturdy yarn, especially if you plan to knit socks you wear outside of the house. Some types of wool are sturdier, like mohair or BFL.
The yarn if sturdier if it more tightly spun.
It also helps to add stronger fibers like silk or nylon\polyester.
It helps to knit with tighter gauge.
If you have a yarn you like, want to knit socks with and it is not strong enough, you can hold a silk mohair with it and knit it at dk weight.
You can also hold a strong thread with it, especially at the heel and toes, to strengthen it even more.
Most people use 20 to 25 percent nylon, and it is probably good to start with.

If you don't like the feeling of defined stitches on your feet? It could help to knit with yarn that felts more, especially 100 percent wool (but 100 percent wool will not hold as well), and knit a sock a little bigger than you want it to end up as.
