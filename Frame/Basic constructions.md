There are two main ways to knit socks - toe up and top down.  For the heel, the main types are heel flap and gusset, short row heel, after thought heel, and fisherman heel. The most popular toe constructions are rounded toe, wedge toe and anatomical toe.
For a standard sock, you need to choose  a direction (toe up or top down), a heel construction and a toe construction.
